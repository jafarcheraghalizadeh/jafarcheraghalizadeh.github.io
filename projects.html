From Single-Chain Dynamics to Structure Formation

Dynamic Self-Consistent Field Theory and Molecular Dynamics of (Co)polymer Melts across Entanglement Regimes

Alireza F. Behbahani, Jafar Cheraghalizadeh, and Friederike Schmid

We connect the dynamics of individual polymer chains to the formation of larger-scale structures in polymer melts. Using molecular dynamics simulations and reptation theory, we construct nonlocal mobility functions for dynamic self-consistent field theory and compare its predictions with molecular dynamics across different entanglement regimes.

The project investigates phase separation in homopolymer blends and diblock copolymer melts, as well as the effects of thermal noise. Mobilities based on single-chain dynamics improve predictions of structure-formation kinetics compared with conventional Debye-type mobilities.
