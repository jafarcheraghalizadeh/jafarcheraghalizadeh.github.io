INTERACTIVE PHYSICS / 02

Explore the Ising model

How does a collection of tiny magnetic spins become ordered? The two-dimensional Ising model is a simple way to explore this question. Each spin points up (+1) or down (−1), and neighboring spins favor alignment.

At low temperature, aligned regions grow. At high temperature, thermal fluctuations disrupt order. Adjust the temperature in the interactive simulation to explore the competition between energy and thermal fluctuations.

The simulation offers square lattices from 16 × 16 to 128 × 128, periodic boundaries, and the Metropolis algorithm. It includes an explanation of the update rule and coding exercises.
